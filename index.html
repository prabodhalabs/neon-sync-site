Cloud Save & Privacy

Why does Neon Sync request Google Play Games access?

To securely save your puzzle progress and daily challenge streaks to the cloud, allowing you to play across multiple devices seamlessly without losing your data.

What data is stored in the cloud?

Only your anonymous game progress (levels completed, hints used, and settings). We do not collect or store any personal information like your name or email.

How do I delete my cloud data?

You can delete your game data at any time from within the Google Play Games app settings. Read our full Terms of Service and Privacy Policy for more details.
